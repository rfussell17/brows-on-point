## 2. What she is doing right (protect these)

1. **The homepage ranks.** It holds page-1 positions for local intent: brow tint near me #8, microblading kelowna #7 to 9, permanent makeup kelowna #7, lash lift kelowna #8, lash lift and tint kelowna #10. The new homepage must keep the same topics, headings and internal links.
2. **Brand strength.** "brows on point kelowna" converts at 44% CTR (position 1.2). Keep the name, address and phone consistent everywhere.
3. **Mobile-first audience.** 81% of clicks are on phones. Large type, fast pages and tap-friendly booking links are the right call.
4. **West Kelowna phrases convert well:** 95 clicks on 1,678 impressions (5.7%).
5. **Testimonials page** gets 876 impressions at position 5.4. Its content now lives in the testimonial sections on the new pages and redirects to the homepage; keep reviews visible.
6. **Price transparency.** Pricing is already on the pages and in the FAQs. Cost queries have impressions but zero clicks, so this is an opportunity, not a loss.

## 3. Biggest finding: searchers say "Kelowna", titles say "West Kelowna"

| Query group (non-brand) | Clicks | Impressions |
|---|---|---|
| contains "kelowna" but not "west kelowna" | 111 | 23,832 |
| contains "west kelowna" | 95 | 1,678 |
| "near me" | 206 | 17,329 |

Every service page title ends in "in West Kelowna". Searchers use "Kelowna" about 14 times more. Fix: lead titles, H1s and meta descriptions with **Kelowna**, and keep West Kelowna as the address and in body copy and schema, for example "Microblading in Kelowna | Brows on Point (West Kelowna)".

## 5. Gaps and opportunities

1. **Cost queries have 441 impressions and 0 clicks** (permanent eyebrows cost, microblading cost, eyebrow tinting cost). Add a visible price and what's included on each service page, and write cost posts (see blog plan).
2. **"brow bar", "eyebrow shaping", "eyebrow services"** have about 3,500 impressions combined. The Brow Tint & Shape page and the Brows hub should use that language in headings.
3. **Lash tint and eyelash lift** have about 8,200 impressions. Strengthen the Lash & Brow Tinting page and add a lash lift cost section.
4. **Skin tightening** has the largest Canada volume in the gap data (1,700) with weak local competition. Build the page out.
5. **Training** pages exist; microblading course queries (200 CA) are a small, separate opportunity only if Jamie trains.
6. **Not worth pursuing:** threading, lamination, nano brows, ombré, lip blushing, lash extensions as services. Cover them only as comparison posts.
